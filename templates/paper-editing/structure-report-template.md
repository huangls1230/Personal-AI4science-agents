## Macro structure

Assess the sections actually present and those appropriate to this manuscript's type. Link each concern to a finding ID and manuscript location. Mark unavailable or unassessable material explicitly; do not infer external research gaps from a single paper.

## Paragraph review

Review every in-scope prose paragraph. Each row identifies its section and line range, and records the topic sentence, supporting evidence, explanation, logical connection, redundancy, placement, and an action: keep, move, merge, split, or add argument. State when a dimension is absent or not assessable.

## Prioritized recommendations

For each finding ID give location and exact original anchor, issue type, specific action, reason, priority, scientific-meaning risk, and whether author approval is required. Keep this consistent with `restructuring_plan.json`.

## Questions and limits

Separate author decisions, unverified scientific claims, missing evidence, and scope limits. Do not label proposals as changes already applied or approvals already given.
