---
name: literature-reading
description: Analyze authorized papers with evidence-linked methods, results, limitations and reading notes; use for research reading rather than citation management.
---

Read only the requested, accessible material. Record whether the evidence comes from metadata, abstract or full text. Separate authors' claims, reported observations and your inference; locate support by page, section, figure or other stable anchor. Explain assumptions, limitations and competing interpretations. If requested, prepare comprehension questions and evaluate only actual answers. Save Markdown notes to the configured workspace and return paths and hashes; users may manually import them into Obsidian. Do not connect to or modify Obsidian.

For the implemented deep-reading workflow, use the project skill `paper-deep-reading` and its referenced procedures; this entry remains a compatibility pointer.
