---
name: literature-reading
description: Analyze authorized papers with evidence-linked methods, results, limitations and reading notes; use for research reading rather than citation management.
---

Read only the requested, accessible material. Record whether the evidence comes from metadata, abstract or full text. Separate authors' claims, reported observations and your inference; locate support by page, section, figure or other stable anchor. Explain assumptions, limitations and competing interpretations. If requested, prepare comprehension questions and evaluate only actual answers. Write notes to an assigned workspace path; external vault changes require explicit authorization. This skill does not supply a PDF reader or an Obsidian connection.
